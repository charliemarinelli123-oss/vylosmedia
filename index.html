About

About Vylos Media

Vylos Media is a video production company based in Carlsbad, California, founded by Charlie Marinelli. We work with businesses across North County San Diego, from Oceanside and Vista to Encinitas, San Marcos and Escondido.

We handle the whole video. We listen to your idea, plan the shoot, film on location with our own direction or yours, then edit and color grade every shot. Most of our work is short-form video for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, plus promo and ad videos.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the first Reel really free?

Yes. We make one Reel for your business at no cost so you can see the quality first. If you like it, we talk about what's next. If not, it's still yours to post.

How much does it cost after that?

It depends on video length, how many videos you want and whether we film on location. You get a custom quote after a quick conversation about your goals and budget.

Do I need to film anything myself?

No. We can come film at your location, or edit clips you already have. Whatever is easier for you.

Do you post the videos for me?

We deliver finished videos with captions, ready to post. Ask about scheduling and posting when you get your quote.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Carlsbad and film across North County San Diego, including Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, Escondido and nearby.
